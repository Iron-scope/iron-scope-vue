Service Agreement

Last updated: August 2026

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Iron Scope LLC, an Oregon limited liability company ("Iron Scope," "we," "us"), and the restoration or mitigation company creating an account ("Client," "you"). By checking the acceptance box during account registration, you agree to be bound by this Agreement.

1. Definitions

"Client" means the restoration or mitigation contracting business that registers an account and engages Iron Scope's services — not any homeowner, property owner, or other insured party. "Policyholder" means the owner of the property for which Client is performing restoration or mitigation work and who holds the applicable insurance policy. "Carrier" means the insurance company, third-party administrator (TPA), or adjuster handling a Policyholder's claim. "Estimate" means the Xactimate scope, ESX file, and supporting documentation Iron Scope prepares under this Agreement.

2. Nature of the Relationship — Not a Public Adjuster

Iron Scope is retained exclusively by Client, a licensed restoration or mitigation contractor, to prepare Estimates. Iron Scope's client relationship is with Client only — Iron Scope does not represent, act on behalf of, or owe any duty to any Policyholder. Iron Scope is not a licensed public adjuster in any jurisdiction and does not perform public adjusting services. Iron Scope does not negotiate, settle, or communicate directly with any Carrier on behalf of Client or any Policyholder regarding the value or settlement of an insurance claim. All communication and negotiation with Carriers regarding a claim is conducted solely by Client, in Client's own name and capacity as the contractor of record. Where Iron Scope's Ironclad tier provides rebuttal or dispute documentation, that documentation is prepared for Client's own use in communications Client conducts directly — Iron Scope does not present, deliver, or advocate that documentation to any Carrier itself.

3. Client's Retained Authority and Responsibility

Client retains full and exclusive authority over every Estimate Iron Scope prepares. Client is solely responsible for reviewing, approving, and submitting each Estimate to the applicable Carrier, and for all representations made to any Carrier or Policyholder in connection with that submission. Iron Scope's role is limited to preparing the Estimate and related documentation at Client's request; Iron Scope has no authority to bind Client, submit an Estimate on Client's behalf, or make representations to any third party regarding a claim.

4. Services

Iron Scope provides independent Xactimate estimate writing and related documentation services for restoration and mitigation contractors, including scope review and verification, insurance-compliant line item documentation, building code and manufacturer research, estimate revisions, supplement preparation, and delivery of a completed ESX file. Our optional Ironclad tier additionally provides guaranteed turnaround times and rebuttal/dispute documentation for Client's own use, as described on our Pricing page and subject to the limitations in Section 2 above.

5. Site Visits

From time to time, and only where agreed in advance with Client, Iron Scope personnel may conduct an on-site visit to a property for purposes of scope verification, measurement, or documentation. Site visits are conducted at Client's request and with Client's confirmation that Iron Scope has authorization to access the property from the Policyholder or property owner; Client is responsible for obtaining that authorization before any visit is scheduled. Iron Scope personnel conducting a site visit do not perform restoration, mitigation, remediation, or repair work, and are not acting as a contractor, subcontractor, or general contractor for the project. Iron Scope is not responsible for the condition of the property, including any pre-existing damage, defect, or hazard not caused by Iron Scope's own conduct during the visit, and Client remains solely responsible for site safety and conditions. Iron Scope maintains commercial general liability insurance covering its site-visit activities and will provide a certificate of insurance upon Client's reasonable request.

Client must disclose to Iron Scope, before a site visit is scheduled, any known hazardous condition at the property, including biohazard, trauma-scene, structural instability, or contamination conditions consistent with IICRC S540 or comparable standards. Iron Scope may decline or reschedule a visit where a disclosed or discovered hazard exceeds what its personnel are equipped to safely address. Client is solely responsible for obtaining the Policyholder's consent to Iron Scope's access to the property and to any photographs, video, or other documentation Iron Scope captures during the visit, and for communicating any site-specific rules or restrictions to Iron Scope in advance.

6. Fees, Deposits, and Payment

Client agrees to pay the estimate writing fee and deposit amounts published on our Pricing page, as may be updated from time to time. A deposit is due at the time a request is submitted and is processed through our third-party payment processor (Stripe). Deposits are non-refundable once work has begun on the associated Estimate, except as required by applicable law. The estimate writing fee is calculated as a percentage of the Carrier-approved Replacement Cost Value (RCV), or the published minimum, whichever is greater, as set out in our current fee schedule. For requests with an estimated RCV of $10,000 or less, the full published minimum fee is collected upfront at submission as the deposit, with no additional amount billed later for that request. For requests above that threshold, the deposit is a portion of the total fee, with the remainder due based on the final Carrier-approved RCV. Ironclad, where purchased, is billed separately as a recurring subscription and is not required for base access to Iron Scope's services.

Iron Scope may offer a limited number of complimentary Estimates to new Client accounts, as described on our Pricing page ("Free Estimates"). Free Estimates include the Estimate itself only. Revisions, supplement preparation, and other services described in Section 4 are not guaranteed on Free Estimates and are provided, if at all, at Iron Scope's sole discretion. Client may request to upgrade a Free Estimate to Ironclad-level support after submission if a Carrier disputes the Estimate; doing so obligates Client to pay the standard estimate writing fee, calculated from the final Carrier-approved RCV, with no deposit required for that specific request.

7. Accurate Reporting of Approved Value

Client agrees to promptly and accurately disclose to Iron Scope the final Carrier-approved RCV for any Estimate Iron Scope prepared, including supporting documentation (approval letter, Estimate of Record, or approved summary) sufficient to verify that value. Iron Scope reserves the right to audit approved claim outcomes and to pursue amounts owed, including through collection or legal action, where Client fails to disclose or misrepresents an approved value.

8. Client Responsibilities

Client is responsible for providing complete and accurate loss documentation, including photographs, moisture readings, 3D scans, and claim information, as described on our Requirements page. Client represents and warrants that it holds all contractor licenses and insurance required to operate in its jurisdiction, and that Client — not Iron Scope — is solely responsible for determining and complying with any licensing requirements applicable to Client's own communications and negotiations with Carriers, including any public adjuster licensing laws that may apply to Client's conduct. Where Iron Scope conducts a site visit under Section 5, Client is responsible for ensuring safe site conditions and for obtaining any access authorization required. Timelines and SLA commitments, where applicable, run from the point Iron Scope has received complete information — not from the date of initial submission if information is incomplete.

9. No Guarantee of Claim Outcome

Estimates prepared by Iron Scope reflect our professional assessment based on the information and documentation provided. Iron Scope does not guarantee that any Carrier will approve an Estimate, in whole or in part, or that a specific RCV will be achieved. Ironclad's SLA guarantees apply only to turnaround time commitments as published, not to claim outcomes or negotiation results, since Iron Scope does not conduct negotiations.

10. Independent Contractor Relationship

Iron Scope acts as an independent contractor in providing services under this Agreement. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship between the parties, and neither party has authority to bind the other. Iron Scope is not Client's agent, representative, or fiduciary, and is not the agent or representative of any Policyholder.

11. Confidentiality

Each party agrees to keep confidential any non-public information disclosed by the other party in connection with this Agreement, including claim details, pricing, and business information, and to use such information solely to perform its obligations under this Agreement. This obligation survives termination of this Agreement.

12. Intellectual Property

Upon full payment, Client owns the specific Estimate and ESX file delivered for that request. Iron Scope retains all rights in its own methodologies, templates, standard rebuttal language, software, and any other materials not specific to a single delivered Estimate. Nothing in this Agreement transfers rights in Iron Scope's underlying tools or business processes to Client.

Iron Scope agrees to indemnify Client for direct damages arising from Iron Scope's gross negligence or willful misconduct in preparing an Estimate, subject to the limitation of liability in Section 14.

Nothing in this Agreement requires either party to indemnify the other for that party's own negligence or willful misconduct to the extent such indemnification is prohibited by applicable law, and any provision of this Agreement shall be interpreted and enforced to the maximum extent permitted under such law.

The limitation in this Section applies to claims arising from site visits under Section 5 to the same extent as any other claim under this Agreement, except that it shall not limit either party's liability for gross negligence, willful misconduct, or bodily injury to the extent such limitation is prohibited by applicable law.

Each party represents that it has the authority to enter into this Agreement and will comply with applicable law in performing its obligations. Client further represents that it will not describe, hold out, or represent Iron Scope as a licensed public adjuster, claims negotiator, or representative of any Policyholder in any communication with a Carrier, Policyholder, or regulator.

Either party may terminate at any time; termination does not relieve Client of payment obligations for services already rendered.
